# Framing revision summary

This revision follows `GFNO-PINO_revision_guide.md`. It changes narrative
framing only in the requested manuscript sections. No equation, methodology
definition, experimental protocol, numerical result, generated table, or
figure was changed.

## Title

- The final title is: "GFNO-PINO: A Topology-Disjoint Benchmark Isolating
  Physics Fine-Tuning, Topology Conditioning, and Spectral Filtering in
  AC-OPF Graph Surrogates."
- The model name identifies the benchmarked instrument, while "isolating"
  foregrounds the controlled comparison among the three design choices.

## Abstract

- Opened with the unresolved comparison between architecturally plausible
  topology/spectral choices and physics-informed fine-tuning.
- Presented GFNO-PINO as the experimental instrument for testing those
  choices, not as a presumed superior solution.
- Moved the validated physics-fine-tuning effect to the main result:
  63.4--89.3% reduction and all 20 paired seed effects positive.
- Placed the negative matched-ablation result immediately after the validated
  finding.
- Retained and strengthened the existing feasibility caveat: every learned
  model has 0% feasibility under the declared proxy.
- Closed by identifying the paper as a controlled-ablation contribution, not a
  production-ready solver.

## Introduction

- Replaced "develops a surrogate" framing with "conducts a controlled,
  topology-disjoint ablation."
- Reordered the contribution list to lead with the AC physics objective and
  benchmark protocol.
- Presented the topology-conditioned Chebyshev model as a hypothesis under
  test.
- Added the validated physics-fine-tuning finding as an explicit contribution.
- Added the unconfirmed topology/spectral effects as an explicit negative
  empirical contribution, together with the 0% feasibility caveat.

## Conclusion

- Opened with the validated finding before describing the architecture.
- Stated immediately that topology-conditioning and Chebyshev benefits are not
  confirmed.
- Recast GFNO-PINO as an instrument for separating effects rather than a
  demonstrated superior architecture.
- Preserved all reported numbers, latency caveats, feasibility limitations,
  and future-work statements.

## Submission material

- Added a one-page cover letter that tells the editor explicitly that this is a
  controlled ablation and benchmark study with a relevant negative result.
